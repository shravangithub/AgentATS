Setup

Live before your coffee cools.

Fully self-provisioning — it creates its own spreadsheet, folders and tabs. You never touch an ID.

STEP 1

Create an Apps Script project

Go to script.google.com and click New project. Free with any Google account.

STEP 2

Paste the files

Copy the six files from the GitHub repo into your project.

STEP 3

Add your Gemini key

One Script Property: GEMINI_KEY, from Google AI Studio. It never lives in code.

STEP 4

Run firstRun & Deploy

One function builds everything and makes you Admin. Deploy as a web app — that URL is your ATS.

~2 minutes. No servers. No bill.

Honest limits

What it's not (yet) — because we'd rather you know.

You're evaluating a hiring system, so here's the full picture up front. Most of these are the deliberate tradeoff of a free tool that runs entirely inside your own Google account.

Google's platform limits

Apps Script has execution caps and daily quotas (API calls, ~100–1,500 emails/day). Very heavy bulk operations can bump them.

AI takes a few seconds

Parsing or scoring a CV isn't instant — expect a few seconds per action. Assistive, not real-time. And AI can misjudge; it assists, it doesn't replace human review.

Best up to mid-size

The database is a Google Sheet: great for hundreds to low-thousands of active candidates, not enterprise volumes. A built-in archiver helps.

Bring a paid Gemini key

Software is free; AI usage is pay-as-you-go on your own key (cents, not seats). Use a paid tier for real candidate data — free tiers may train on inputs.

Not SOC 2 certified

No SOC 2 / ISO certifications, no third-party pen test. Security is app-level and depends on your Google account hygiene. Regulated buyers need more.

Single workspace, no job boards

One company per install, no SSO/SCIM, and no built-in LinkedIn/Indeed integrations — intake is your careers page, CV-by-email, or manual.

In one line: the most capable ATS you can run for free inside your own Google account, with real AI — built for startups and lean TA teams, not regulated enterprises. Community open-source means no SLA: you self-host, you self-support, and you own everything.
